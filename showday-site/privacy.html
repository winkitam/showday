Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short

We collect only what is needed to run a fan-to-fan noticeboard. We never sell your data, we show no ads, and we never see payments. Your email is never shown to other users.

1. Who we are

Show Day is an independent fan project run from London, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: showday.cs@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our login provider; we cannot read them.

Profile: the nickname, animal avatar and colour you choose. These are public.

Your posts: ticket listings, seat swaps, buddy posts, merch posts and freebies, including any text, prices, seat details, meeting times and places, and photos you upload. Posts and your profile are visible to anyone. The contact handle you add to a ticket listing is shown only to logged-in users.

Reports and feedback: reports you make about posts, feedback you send, and a reply email if you choose to give one.

Technical data: basic logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and to keep the site running.

On your device: we use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and remember your language, theme and filters. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics.

We do not collect payment details. Show Day does not handle money between users. If we add paid features later, we will update this policy before we collect any payment data.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

To run the service you asked for (contract): creating your account, showing your posts, letting fans contact each other.

To keep the board safe (legitimate interests and legal obligation): enforcing the face-value rule, reviewing reports, removing harmful content, preventing fraud and spam, and meeting our duties under the Online Safety Act 2023.

To reply to you (legitimate interests): answering feedback and support emails.

4. Who can see it, and who we share it with

Other users can see your public posts and profile. We do not sell or rent your data. We use these service providers to run Show Day:

Supabase (database, login and photo storage) and Cloudflare (website hosting). They process data only on our instructions. Some of their servers may be outside the UK; where that happens, they use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

We may share information with the police or other authorities if the law requires it, or to protect someone from fraud or harm.

5. How long we keep it

Posts and photos: kept for up to 6 months after the show date (so fans can look back at past shows), then deleted.

Account and profile: kept until you ask us to delete your account.

Reports and moderation records: up to 12 months, so we can deal with repeat abuse.

Feedback emails: up to 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can delete your own posts at any time. To delete your account or use any of these rights, email showday.cs@gmail.com from your account email. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Age

Show Day is for people aged 16 and over. If you are under 16, please do not create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it.

8. Keeping you safe

Only share what you need to. Do not post your phone number, home address, or a ticket's QR code or barcode in public. Meet in busy places near the venue.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will also show a notice on the site.
