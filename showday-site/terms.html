4. The face-value rule

Tickets on Show Day must be offered at the original face value or less. The face value is the price printed on the ticket or charged by the official seller, including any booking fee you actually paid. You must not:

  • ask for more than face value, including through "extra fees", bundled items, or side payments;
  • list a ticket you do not have, or list the same ticket twice;
  • post tickets as part of a ticket resale business, or bought in order to resell.

When you post a ticket, state the face value, the section or seat if you know it, and any restrictions you are aware of (for example ID checks or name transfer rules). This information is required by UK consumer law when tickets are resold.

5. Organiser rules

Many organisers restrict ticket transfer, require ID that matches the ticket, or cancel tickets that are resold. It is your responsibility to check the organiser's terms before you transfer or accept a ticket. Show Day is not responsible if an organiser refuses entry or cancels a ticket.

6. Staying safe

  • Never post a ticket's QR code, barcode or booking reference in public. Blur them in photos.
  • Prefer to hand over tickets in person near the venue, or use the official transfer system.
  • Be careful with bank transfers to strangers. Never pay "friends and family" for someone you have not met.
  • Meet in busy, public places. Tell a friend where you are going.
  • Do not share your phone number, home address or ID documents in posts.

If you think you have been scammed, report it to your bank and to Action Fraud (actionfraud.police.uk) in the UK, or the local police where you are, and report the post to us.
